How it works

  1. Drop a file. MP4, MOV, WebM, M4A, MP3, WAV or OGG. Up to your browser’s limits.
  2. Choose the spoken language and the task. Transcribe keeps the original language. Translate to English uses Whisper’s built-in speech-to-English mode.
  3. Press Transcribe. Whisper runs in this tab. The waveform, transcript and timestamps appear as it listens.
  4. Export. Copy, or download as TXT, SRT or VTT. Use the Video button to save the source file.

Private by design

Everything runs on your device. Your audio and video are decoded in the browser and transcribed by a model that downloads once and then runs offline. Nothing is uploaded, no account is needed, and no one else can read your files. That makes Tannscribe a good fit for journalists, students, researchers and creators working with sensitive material.

Supported formats and exports

  • Input: MP3, MP4, WAV, M4A, MOV, WebM, OGG and most browser-readable containers.
  • Transcript: plain text with timestamps, one line per segment.
  • Subtitles: SRT and WebVTT, ready for YouTube, Premiere, CapCut, ffmpeg and most players.
  • Languages: 98, read from the Whisper model list. Transcription works in all of them; English translation runs offline.

Frequently asked questions

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. The model runs in your browser tab. Your file never leaves your device.

Does it cost anything?

No. Whisper is open source and runs locally. Optional translation to other languages uses your own free Google Gemini API key, which you paste into the panel. We never see it.

Why is the first run slow?

The first run downloads the model (about 94 MB) and caches it. After that, transcription runs offline. On a modest CPU, expect roughly one hour of processing per hour of audio.

Can I translate into languages other than English?

Whisper translates into English only. For other languages, use the collapsible panel below the transcript with your own Gemini key. The SRT timestamps are preserved.

What about very long files?

Long files work but take time. Keep the tab open and the device awake until it finishes.

Are third-party services used?

Transcription and English translation run locally. The optional Gemini translator sends only the transcript text to Google, using your own key. The page also shows ads served by a third-party network, which may set cookies. No audio or video file is ever uploaded.
